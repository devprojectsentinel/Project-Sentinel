01 // Operational architecture

How Project Sentinel works in three steps

Built for everyday citizens and non-technical staff. When an emergency happens you do not need to remember hotline numbers or explain your address under stress.

TIER 01 // CITIZEN MOBILE APP ANDROID BYOD

Swipe for help — or trigger silently

Tap the agency you need (Police, Fire, Ambulance, or Barangay) and slide the emergency bar. Your phone transmits live GPS, your registered building/floor/room, and live video and audio from the camera.

Silent duress & blackout mode: If an intruder is nearby, slide left (or enter your Duress PIN). The screen goes pitch black and silent while streaming video and mic audio to authorities.
TIER 02 // GUARDHOUSE KIOSK ON-SITE TABLET

Immediate on-property security response

Your subdivision guardhouse, school security office, or building lobby tablet sounds an alarm within one second—showing exact unit number and live camera so guards can reach your door and clear the gate for incoming responders.

30-second unattended fail-safe: If a guardhouse tablet is left unanswered for 30 seconds, the emergency escalates automatically to government authorities.
TIER 03 // RESPONDER CONSOLE PNP · BFP · EMS · BRGY

Coordinated public safety dispatch

Local Police, Fire, Ambulance, and Barangay stations receive your live map pin, indoor room details, and live camera stream at the same time. When a responder clicks Acknowledge & En Route, your phone updates so you know help is coming.

Automatic local evidence recording: The station computer records the live video and audio stream to its local hard drive (.webm) for post-incident investigation.
02 // Capability comparison

Project Sentinel vs. traditional Philippine e911

Voice hotlines require callers to speak out loud, describe landmarks, and wait through transfers. Sentinel removes those delays while keeping an automatic 911 dialer fallback when you are outside partnered municipalities.

Emergency factor Traditional PH e911 / voice hotline Project Sentinel
Time to alert local station 3 to 7+ minutes. Wait for a regional call-taker, explain the emergency, then wait for the report to be relayed by radio or phone. Under 1 second. Alerts go directly to your property guardhouse and bound Barangay, PNP, BFP, and EMS stations at once.
Indoor & multi-floor accuracy Verbal directions only. Cell-tower location cannot tell responders which wing, floor, unit, or stall you are in. Verified room profile + live GPS. Shows Complex/Mall, Building, Floor, Room/Stall, and Purok alongside moving GPS.
Visual & audio verification Voice call only. Dispatchers cannot see the scale of a fire, injury, or threat unless the caller can describe it clearly. Live one-way video & audio. Guardhouses and stations see and hear the scene and can record evidentiary video locally.
Hostage, break-in & domestic safety Dangerous if overheard. Dialing and speaking can put a victim at risk when an intruder is nearby. Silent duress blackout. One silent swipe blacks out the screen and streams camera and mic evidence with no sound or light.
Property security coordination Disconnected. Guards often learn of a 911 call only when police or fire arrive locked out at the main gate. Synchronized gatekeeper kiosk. Property guards are alerted first so they can respond and open gates for emergency vehicles.
Cost to local government High capital spend. Legacy command centers need expensive servers, proprietary licenses, and long procurement cycles. ₱0.00 for public safety agencies. Cross-subsidized by private institutions; runs on any standard station PC browser.
03 // Target deployment sectors

Who Project Sentinel is designed for

Built to link private residential, academic, and commercial properties directly with local government responder stations on a shared map.

Sector 01 // Residential

Subdivisions, condominiums & villages

Gives homeowners and condo residents a direct line to their village guardhouse and local Barangay patrol, with exact block, lot, tower, and unit numbers.

Sector 02 // Education

Schools, colleges & university campuses

Lets faculty, students, and campus security report medical emergencies, fires, or threats while broadcasting lockdown or evacuation alerts across campus phones.

Sector 03 // Commercial

Shopping malls, banks & retail strips

Equips cashiers, tenants, and bank staff with a silent duress trigger that alerts mall security and the nearest PNP station without tipping off perpetrators.

Sector 04 // Enterprise

Corporate towers, BPOs & industrial parks

Pinpoints the exact building wing and floor during workplace medical or hazmat incidents so paramedics arrive at the right elevator bank.

Sector 05 // Public safety

PNP, BFP, EMS & Barangay stations

Provides a ₱0.00 web dispatch console with live video verification, one-click Google Maps routing, and COA-compliant audit logs.

Sector 06 // Local government

City & municipal DRRMO command centers

Unifies barangay halls and municipal emergency agencies under one command umbrella without cloud video storage fees or hardware lock-in.

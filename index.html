01 // Operational Architecture

How Project Sentinel Works in Three Steps

Designed for everyday citizens and non-technical personnel. When an emergency happens, you do not need to remember hotline numbers or explain your address under stress.

TIER 01 // CITIZEN MOBILE APP ANDROID BYOD

Swipe for Help — Or Trigger Silently

Tap the agency you need (Police, Fire, Ambulance, or Barangay) and slide the emergency bar. Your phone immediately transmits your live GPS pin, your registered building, floor, and room number, and live video and audio from your camera.

Silent Duress & Blackout Mode: If an intruder or attacker is inside the room, slide the bar left (or enter your Duress PIN). Your phone screen turns completely pitch black and silent while streaming live video and microphone audio straight to authorities.
TIER 02 // GUARDHOUSE KIOSK ON-SITE TABLET

Immediate On-Property Security Response

Your subdivision guardhouse, school security office, or building lobby tablet sounds an alarm within one second—showing your exact unit number and live camera feed so on-site guards can rush to your door and clear the main gate for incoming responders.

30-Second Unattended Fail-Safe: If a guardhouse tablet is left unattended for 30 seconds, Project Sentinel automatically escalates your emergency straight to government authorities without waiting.
TIER 03 // RESPONDER CONSOLE PNP · BFP · EMS · BRGY

Coordinated Public Safety Dispatch

Local Police, Fire, Ambulance, and Barangay stations receive your live map pin, indoor room details, and live camera stream at the same time. The moment a responder clicks Acknowledge & En Route, your phone updates so you know help is coming.

Automatic Local Evidence Recording: The station computer records the live video and audio stream directly to its local hard drive (.webm) for official post-incident investigation.
02 // Capability Comparison

Project Sentinel vs. Traditional Philippine e911 Calls

Voice hotlines require callers to speak out loud, describe landmarks verbally, and wait through call-center transfers. Project Sentinel removes those delays while keeping an automatic 911 dialer fallback when you travel outside partnered municipalities.

Time to Alert Local Station 3 to 7+ minutes. Callers wait for a regional call-taker, explain the emergency verbally, and wait for the report to be relayed by radio or phone to the local station. Under 1 second. Alerts route directly to your property guardhouse and your bound Barangay, PNP, BFP, and EMS stations simultaneously.
Indoor & Multi-Floor Accuracy Verbal directions only. Standard cell tower location cannot tell responders which building wing, floor level, condo unit, or mall stall you are inside. Verified room profile + Live GPS. Displays your exact Complex/Mall, Building, Floor, Room/Stall, and Purok alongside your live moving GPS coordinates.
Visual & Audio Verification Voice call only. Dispatchers cannot see the scale of a fire, medical injury, or armed threat unless the caller can describe it clearly. Live 1-Way Video & Audio Stream. Guardhouses and responder stations see and hear the scene live and record evidentiary video locally.

Silent Duress Blackout Protocol. One silent swipe blacks out the phone screen and streams live camera and microphone evidence without a sound or light.
